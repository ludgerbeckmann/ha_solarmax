# Archived original brand images

These three images are the SolarMax brand images that earlier versions of this
integration shipped as its icon and logo. They are kept here only as a
maintainer reference.

- They are **not** part of the integration: they live outside
  `custom_components/`, are not installed by HACS and are not loaded by Home
  Assistant.
- They are **not** covered by this repository's MIT license. The SolarMax
  name and logo belong to their respective rights holders, and this project is
  not affiliated with them.
- The integration itself uses its own neutral icon in
  `custom_components/ha_solarmax/brand/`.
